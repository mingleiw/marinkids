Outdoor activities for kids near Tiburon

Parks, beaches, trails and open-air attractions near Tiburon — 8 spots within 30 miles.

Marin Headlands & Rodeo Beach

Sausalito, Marin County

Wide-open headlands, a dark-pebble beach, old military bunkers to explore, and the postcard view back at the bridge.

  • 5.2 mi
  • 3 and up
  • Outdoors

Before you go Fog and hard wind are the norm, not the exception. The road in is narrow and winding — worth knowing if anyone gets carsick. No shade and limited facilities, so bring water.

Muir Woods National Monument

Mill Valley, Marin County

Old-growth coast redwoods on a flat boardwalk loop. Quiet, shaded, and short enough that small legs can do it.

  • 6.4 mi
  • 3 and up
  • Outdoors

Before you go Parking or shuttle must be reserved in advance — people are turned away at the gate every weekend. The main boardwalk is stroller-friendly; the side trails are not. Cool and damp even in August.

Map & reservations

Koret Children’s Quarter

Golden Gate Park, San Francisco

One of the oldest public playgrounds in the country, with a century-old carousel and a bank of long concrete slides.

  • 7.2 mi
  • 1–10
  • Outdoors

Before you go Bring a flattened cardboard box — the concrete slides barely work without one, and every regular family has one in the trunk. Weekend parking is tight; transit is genuinely easier.

Map & parking

San Francisco Zoo

Sunset District, San Francisco

A dedicated children’s zoo and a little steam train, which is often the part they actually remember.

  • 10 mi
  • Under 10
  • Outdoors

Before you go It sits directly on the Pacific and runs far colder and foggier than downtown, in summer especially. Bring a real jacket — this is the single most common mistake here.

Tilden Regional Park

Berkeley Hills, East Bay

The Little Farm, a vintage merry-go-round and a small steam railway, all in one stretch of the Berkeley hills. A rite of passage for East Bay kids.

  • 12 mi
  • Under 10
  • Outdoors

Before you go At the Little Farm you can feed the animals, but only lettuce and celery — bring it from home, there’s nothing to buy on site. The train and carousel keep their own limited schedules, so check before counting on them.

Map & directions

Children’s Fairyland

Lake Merritt, Oakland

A storybook park built entirely at toddler scale — nothing loud, nothing scary, no long lines.

  • 12 mi
  • 1–8
  • Outdoors

Before you go Adults are only admitted with a child — don’t plan to send one grown-up in to scout. It’s a half-day park; don’t block out the whole day.

Oakland Zoo

Oakland

A gondola lifts you over the whole zoo and out across the bay — worth the trip on its own.

  • 19 mi
  • All ages
  • Outdoors

Before you go Built on a hillside, so a stroller is real work. The East Bay bakes in summer while SF is foggy — hats, sunscreen and more water than you think.

Half Moon Bay & the Coastside

Half Moon Bay, Peninsula

Long flat beaches, a working harbour, tidepools and pumpkin fields in autumn. The classic coastal day out.

  • 28 mi
  • All ages
  • Outdoors

Before you go Cold and windy even in summer — this is not a swimming beach, it’s a digging-and-running beach. Highway 92 and Highway 1 back up badly on weekend afternoons, and October is gridlock. Never turn your back on the surf.
