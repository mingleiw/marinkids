Before you go

Days out with kids rarely fall apart over the activity itself

  • Confirm before you drive

    Weekly sessions get cancelled and hours change. This site links straight to each venue's map listing, which is always current.

  • Let the fog set the timetable

    The marine layer can sit over the coast until noon while Novato and San Rafael run ten degrees warmer. Mornings near the water want layers; inland afternoons want sunscreen.

  • Solve parking in advance

    Muir Woods requires a reserved parking spot or shuttle seat — no drive-ups. On sunny weekends, popular trailhead and beach lots fill by late morning, so come early or plan to walk.

  • Plan one thing, not three

    Most of these fill a half day. Leaving room for a nap and some nothing-in-particular beats cramming in another stop.
