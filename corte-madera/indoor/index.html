Indoor activities for kids near Corte Madera

Play spaces, museums and indoor activities near Corte Madera — 5 options within 30 miles.

Exploratorium

Pier 15, San Francisco

Hundreds of hands-on exhibits you’re actually supposed to touch, pull and climb on. Adults tend to lose track of time here.

  • 11 mi
  • 6 and up
  • Indoors

Before you go It’s enormous and you will walk — comfortable shoes matter. Under-5s can enjoy it but will miss most of the point. It’s on the water, so it’s windy outside the doors.

Map, hours & tickets

California Academy of Sciences

Golden Gate Park, San Francisco

Aquarium, four-story rainforest dome, planetarium and natural history museum under one roof. The default answer when it’s raining.

  • 11 mi
  • All ages
  • Indoors

Before you go The rainforest dome runs on timed entry and the line builds fast — go early. Weekend parking in the park is rough; the underground garage is the reliable option.

Map, hours & tickets

Children’s Creativity Museum

Yerba Buena Gardens, San Francisco

Stop-motion animation studio, clay lab and music room. Kids leave holding something they made.

  • 12 mi
  • 3–9
  • Indoors

Before you go Small enough for a half day. Pair it with the historic carousel and the gardens right outside to make a full outing.

Map, hours & tickets

Randall Museum

Corona Heights, San Francisco

A small hilltop community museum with live animals, craft workshops and one of the better views in the city.

  • 12 mi
  • Under 10
  • Indoors

Before you go Compact — plan a half day and add the hill outside. It closes on set days each week, so check before driving over.

Map & hours

Chabot Space & Science Center

Oakland Hills

Planetarium shows, space exhibits and large research telescopes up in the hills above Oakland.

  • 20 mi
  • 6 and up
  • Indoors

Before you go Telescope viewing runs on specific evenings and depends entirely on clear skies — check the schedule and the forecast together, and bring warm layers for standing outside after dark.
